EmpiriQal estimates how likely a published scientific claim is to replicate. Paste a paper's abstract or DOI and the claim you care about, and you get a report with the estimated probability that the finding would hold up in a new study, alongside related papers that support or contradict it. Reports can be shared with a link, or made citable with a DOI. Evaluations are also available through an API.

Every account starts with 3 free evaluations. After that, a membership gives you credits each month: one evaluation uses one credit.
