Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains what EmpiriQal, Inc. (“EmpiriQal”, “we”), Santa Fe, NM 87505, USA, collects when you use EmpiriQal, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and your rights. We are the controller of this information.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture Google shares with us.
  • What you submit: abstracts, claims, original findings, DOIs and titles, and the reports we produce from them.
  • Your use of the service: your evaluations and when you ran them, your credits, memberships and purchases, share links and DOI requests.
  • Payments: handled by Paddle, our reseller, which sells memberships and credits to you on our behalf and processes your payment details under its own privacy notice. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as the card type and last four digits, never the full card number.
  • Technical records: records of errors and failed requests, linked to your account, which we use to find and fix problems; and the standard records our hosting keeps of requests to the site, such as IP addresses.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service: run your evaluations, keep your history, share reports and register DOIs you ask for (performing our contract with you).
  • To take payments, keep accounts and meet tax and legal duties (contract and legal obligation).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse such as repeated free accounts, and find and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
  • To improve the service, including from evaluations you have deleted and anonymised copies of evaluations from deleted accounts (our legitimate interests).
  • To tell you about changes to the service or these terms (contract and legitimate interests).

Who else handles it

We use service providers who process information on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us:

  • cloud hosting, database and sign-in providers;
  • an AI service provider, which processes what you submit to produce estimates, and may not use it to train its models;
  • a scholarly literature database, which receives the text of your submission to find related papers;
  • Paddle, our reseller, for memberships and credits;
  • an email delivery provider, for sign-in links and receipts.

Reports you share or register. Anyone with a share link can see that report. If you request a DOI, the report becomes public, and its title, date and DOI are sent to a DOI registration agency and published in its records. Shared reports and DOI records never include your name or email.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or others' safety.

Where it is processed

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. When information from the UK or the European Union is transferred, we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and evaluations: while your account exists.
  • Evaluations you delete are removed from your history but kept, and may be used to improve the service, until you delete your account.
  • Records of errors and failed requests: 90 days.
  • Payment and tax records: as long as the law requires.
  • Reports with a DOI: permanently, as part of the scholarly record.

Cookies and storage

We store only what is needed to keep you signed in and remember small settings in your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you and get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable form. To get a copy of your data, use “Request a copy of your data” on the Plan and usage page of your account; for anything else, contact us. We reply within one month, and we may need to confirm your identity first. If you are in the UK or the European Union, you may also complain to your data protection authority, for example the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.

You can delete your account yourself from the Plan and usage page of your account. This erases your account and evaluations straight away, except reports with a DOI, which are permanent. We keep an anonymised copy of each evaluation (what was submitted and the result, dated to the day), with nothing that links it to you or your account, and may use it to improve the service. We keep a one-way scrambled form of your email address, from which the address can't be read back, only so that it does not receive free evaluations again; records of errors expire after 90 days.

Children

EmpiriQal is not for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or on the site before the change applies.
